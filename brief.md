Title:  

  From agent experimentation to governed scale

Summary:

  Learn how Point32Health can evaluate the visibility, governance, security, and operational controls needed to scale AI agents responsibly through a structured, evidence-based proof of concept.

Description:  

  Point32Health is already operating at meaningful agent scale, with agents being created and distributed faster than manual oversight can comfortably support. As adoption grows, leaders need to know which agents exist, who owns them, what data and connectors they use, how access and sharing are controlled, and whether security teams can investigate risky activity efficiently.

  This session presents a practical framework for evaluating Microsoft Agent 365 alongside Point32Health's existing identity, governance, data protection, and security investments. Rather than relying on a feature walkthrough, the proposed proof of concept uses representative Copilot and Foundry agents, agreed scenarios, and measurable outcomes to determine what becomes more visible, enforceable, and easier to investigate.

  Attendees will see how complete visibility, controlled participation, actionable protection, and confident scale can turn agent governance from an assumption into a defensible business decision. Whether the evidence supports broader adoption or confirms that existing controls are sufficient, the goal is the same: scale where value is proven and avoid unnecessary investment where it is not.
